TripAdvisor tells you what strangers liked. This knows what you liked.

Palate turns the reservations, cancellations, calendar entries, and saved lists you already made into a taste graph—then plans the version of a city that behaves like you.

Revealed behavior beats declared preference. Distaste is a signal, too.

01 / Profile

A travel product that notices.

  3. You go back. 22 visits across 13 places — 15 of them to the same 6.
  4. You have never scheduled anything before 11am. Not once in 21 completed visits.
  5. You cancel at the top of your price range. 4 of 5 bookings at band 4.
  6. You sit at the bar. Every one of 2 solo reservations was a bar seat.
  7. You saved 3 places to a list. You have been to none of them.

03 / System

The restraint is the intelligence.

01

Transactional history becomes revealed taste.

Sender-scoped Gmail and Calendar extraction produces normalized visits. Source-counted SQL—not a model—computes every profile metric.

02

Aversion filters run before venue ranking.

Cancellation bands, one-and-done cuisines, and saved-never-visited categories cannot sneak back in through popularity.

03

One gateway controls every model call.

Extraction and rationale generation share a Merge Gateway wrapper; direct-provider mode is isolated as an explicit emergency fallback.
