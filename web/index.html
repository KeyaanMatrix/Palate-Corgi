Profile

  • Earliest out: 11:00 — fifth percentile of your booked hours.
  • Peak dinner: 8pm on Tue/Wed, party of two.
  • Price ceiling: $$$; cancel rate spikes above that band.
  • Seat: bar/counter — 19 of 27 dinners where seat is known.

How it works

  1. Taste profile is pure SQL over your real bookings — every number traces to a row count.
  2. iMessage tapbacks are the control surface: 👎 swaps one stop; the rest of the day stays put.
  3. Mid-trip state (“it’s raining”) re-plans from now forward without rewriting locked stops.

What’s real

Profile metrics and visit rows are sourced from the presenter’s inbox where the pipeline completed; city candidates and a few swap alternatives may be seeded so the live demo cannot die on venue wifi. The iMessage thread, tapback swap, and keyword re-plan are the real product path.
